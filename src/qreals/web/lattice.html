Brick lattice

The divisor lattice of d, each node the cyclotomic brick \(\Phi_e\) for a divisor \(e \ge 2\), marked kept, dropped, or repeated in the denominator \(S(q)\) of \(\left[\tfrac{a}{d}\right]_q\). Default example: d = 60.
